Good to know

Momo's Hunt FAQ

What time does Momo's Hunt close?

Dine-in runs until 1:30am and take-away until 2:00am, every day of the week. We open at 1pm Sunday to Friday and 2pm on Saturday, so we're one of the latest kitchens open in Granville and Parramatta.

Is there parking at Momo's Hunt Granville?

Yes. Free parking anytime on McGill La, directly behind the restaurant at 111 Parramatta Rd. We're also about a six-minute walk from Granville Station.

Do you have halal food?

Yes, halal options are available across the menu, including chicken momo, choila, khana sets and chowmein. Just ask when you order.

How do I book a table or a function?

Use the Book a table page to pick a date, time and guest numbers. A $10 deposit is paid by PayID ($10 per head for function and event bookings) and we confirm by WhatsApp or phone on (02) 9683 7306.

Do you do catering for parties and pujas?

Yes. We do momo trays (50 pieces), khana trays, sekuwa platters and chowmein trays, plus set catering menus from $22 a head. Please order at least 48 hours ahead.

Can I order momo for pick-up or delivery?

Yes. Build your order on the menu and send it to us on WhatsApp for pick-up in about 20 minutes, or order through DoorDash and Uber Eats when the links are live.
